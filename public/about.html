Our Story

Christ Ascension Church

Foundation

Lifters Power Assembly, since 1984

Christ Ascension Church — Lifters Power Assembly — was established on the 1984 Foundation Constitution, built on the Great Commission of Mark 16:15: "Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature."

For over four decades the assembly has gathered its members — the "Lifters" — around that same charge: to lift up the name of Christ and to lift one another in fellowship, prayer, and service.

"Hitherto hath the Lord helped us." — 1 Samuel 7:12

Every few years, the whole assembly is called together from its districts into one camp for the Unity Convention — a season set apart from the ordinary calendar for worship, the Word, and the kind of fellowship that can only happen when the household of faith is under one roof.

About EBENEZER 2026

EBENEZER 2026 is this cycle's Unity Convention — four days of camp gathering under the theme of remembrance and thanksgiving. This site was built so that members who could not travel to camp, and members who simply want to relive it, can still walk through the convention in pictures: the opening night, the revival services, the testimonies, and the closing send-forth.
