Why we call it Ebenezer

In Scripture, Israel raised a stone of remembrance after every deliverance — not to worship the stone, but to mark the place where help had come. EBENEZER 2026 is our stone: a gathering of the whole assembly to look back on the Lord's faithfulness since our foundation in 1984, and to look forward together in unity.
